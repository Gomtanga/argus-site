Features

Research that knows its own limits

Argus v4.5 goes beyond search volume with a framework designed for conservative claims, source quality, and transparent uncertainty.

4-Phase Search Framework

Broad exploration, targeted investigation, cross-verification, and comparative analysis — each query builds on the last.

Evidence Calibration

Major claims are labeled as Verified, Supported, Anecdotal, Unverified, or Speculative before synthesis.

Numeric Claim Discipline

Numbers are used only when directly relevant, source-verifiable, dated, and tied to the claim they support.

Claim Coverage Matrix

Research reports show which claims have primary-source coverage and which need downgrading or verification.

Source Tiering

Sources are sorted from S-tier primary evidence to D-tier weak leads, preventing weak sources from sounding authoritative.

Strong Language Guardrails

Terms like standard, production-proven, verified, and community consensus require qualifying evidence.

How It Works

Systematic depth, calibrated conclusions

Argus follows a structured 4-phase framework, then downgrades claims when the evidence is weak or indirect.

Principles

What Argus will never do

Clear rules prevent common AI research failures and overconfident reports.

✕

Assert facts without verification through search

✕

Make major claims based on a single source

✕

Force numbers just to satisfy a metric quota

✕

Call something standard, verified, or production-proven without qualifying evidence

Get Started

Up and running in minutes

Argus works as an OpenCode agent skill. Install it, trigger it with natural language, and let it research conservatively.

1

Prerequisites

You need OpenCode and Firecrawl access through the CLI, Python SDK, or direct HTTP.

2

Install the Skill

Install the v4.5 skill files in your OpenCode skills directory, including the references/ folder.

3

Just Ask

Use natural language: "Compare React vs Vue performance in 2026 and flag weak evidence" — Argus handles the rest.
